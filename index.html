About Us

A family-owned team you can trust

We're not a franchise or a call center. When you call Chinners Heating and Air, you talk to the people who actually show up at your door.

Honesty first. Always.

Founded in 2021 in Summerville, South Carolina, Chinners Heating and Air was built on a simple promise: give our neighbors the same honest, no-pressure service we'd want for our own families.

We specialize in residential HVAC systems with deep expertise in geothermal heat pumps. Whether it's a quick repair, a full system replacement, or a brand-new install, we show up on time, explain everything in plain language, and leave your home cleaner than we found it.

What We Do

Full-service HVAC for your home

From emergency repairs to complete system installs, we handle every part of your comfort system.

Our Specialty

Geothermal Heat Pumps

Our specialty. Energy-efficient geothermal installation and repair for homeowners who want the lowest operating costs and the longest-lasting system on the market — the smartest long-term choice for a Lowcountry home.

AC & Heating Repair

Fast, honest diagnostics for all major brands. We find the real problem — not just the symptom — and give you a fair quote before any work begins.

System Replacement

Full AC and heating unit replacements, matched to your home's needs and budget. We handle old-system removal and the new install, start to finish.

Thermostats

Smart thermostat installation and configuration. Get precise temperature control and real energy savings from a system you can run from your phone.

Maintenance & Tune-Ups

Regular maintenance keeps your system at peak efficiency and catches small problems before they become expensive ones. Seasonal checkups included.

Duct Work

Leaky ducts waste energy and money. We inspect, repair, and install ductwork so conditioned air actually reaches every room in your home.
