About us

A family-owned team you can trust

We're not a franchise or a call center. When you call Chinners Heating and Air, you talk to the people who actually show up at your door — Jeffrey, Meredith, Jennifer and Sean.

Founded in 2021 in Summerville, South Carolina, we were built on a simple promise: give our neighbors the same honest, no-pressure service we'd want for our own families. We specialize in residential HVAC with deep expertise in geothermal heat pumps — whether it's a quick repair, a full replacement, or a brand-new install.
